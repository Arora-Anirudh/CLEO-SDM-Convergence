# Work log - 2026-07-31

## Prospective fixed-50 Golovin extension

The researcher accepted a numerical, uncertainty-aware practical
diminishing-returns framework and explicitly rejected retaining a ratio of
successive error reductions, even as a secondary statistic.

A new experiment definition and immutable 450-row matrix were prepared:

```text
golovin_fixed50_extended_resolution_convergence_v1
```

The design contains 50 independent collision streams at each of nine
factor-of-two resolutions from 4,096 through 1,048,576 SDs.  It uses fresh
collision seeds, run labels, run paths and experiment-specific controlled
bundle labels.  No model data have been generated and the matrix manifest
still records `submission_authorized=false`.

Before submission, the new experiment's full-state output cadence was reduced
prospectively from 300 to 600 s.  Every formal decision is already registered
at 600-s intervals, so this removes unused intermediate states without
changing collision physics or an estimand.

The practical decision keeps the prospectively defined one-percentage-point
minimum worthwhile improvement, requires one-sided 95% bootstrap upper bounds
inside that margin for two consecutive doublings, and checks stability
between the 40- and 50-member prefixes.  The 250/1000-bin products remain
sensitivity diagnostics.

Added `analyze_golovin_convergence_law.py`.  It independently bootstraps
members and fits

\[
E(N)=E_\infty+a(N/N_{\min})^{-p}
\]

over the highest four, five and six levels for L1 and absolute \(M_0\)/\(M_6\)
bias.  The fit reports floor, exponent and projected next-doubling
uncertainty, but is explicitly prevented from selecting a resolution.
The standard resolution-analysis Slurm entrypoint now runs the practical and
convergence-law stages in the same audited allocation when their reviewed
YAML sections are present.

Measured fixed-10 costs imply about 55--61 physical member CPU-hours and
26--29 GB of new raw SCRATCH for the full fixed-50 ladder including the new
1,048,576-SD level.  The live `m301324` filesystem has about 15 TB free and no
current user job.  This is a capacity check, not submission authorization.
